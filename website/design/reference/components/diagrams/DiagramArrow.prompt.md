DiagramArrow is the only connector allowed in Convoy diagrams; DiagramLegend explains the three line kinds.

```jsx
<DiagramArrow kind="execution" />
<DiagramArrow kind="release" />
<DiagramLegend />
```

- Solid ink = execution data/commands. Dashed oxide = release, configuration, evidence. Dotted muted = optional, configuration-dependent placement.
- Never colour-code by product or vendor; grammar is by line style only.
